Spatial UIAR / VRCourse-end Project

ABC Cars — AR & VR Booking

ABC Inc.'s booking site relied on static images and text-heavy listings, so users hesitated — especially on group bookings where SUV and bus size matters. This project lets users place a vehicle in their real surroundings (AR) and explore a 3D showroom (VR) before they book.

PersonasFamily traveler · Corporate group coordinator
FlowSearch → Listing → Detail → AR or VR → Booking
ToolsFigma · Spline · ShapesXR
Scope6 mobile screens (375×812) in one clickable prototype
Open prototype ↗

Spline showroom

A lit 3D scene framed at a 3/4 hero angle, with a click-to-rotate interaction so users can inspect the car from every side.

ShapesXR layout

The VR dashboard was placed at eye level in front of a human-scale avatar: vehicle stage first, colour panel in reach, navigation closest to the user.

Usability finding

The biggest risk is discoverability — AR/VR entry points rely on small icons, so labelled invitations would help.
