ABOUT ME
The story so far.

I wasn't always a designer. I spent three years as a Configuration & Testing Analyst at Wipro, configuring benefits systems for ExxonMobil's U.S. retirement program — where I learned that getting a system right means deeply understanding the people who depend on it.

That curiosity about how things work for real people led me to UI/UX design. Today I'm completing an Advanced Certification in UI/UX Design with Generative AI from IIITB × Simplilearn, building a portfolio that bridges both worlds.

During college at NCERC, I served as Chairperson of the IEEE PES Student Branch — which taught me that the best designs, like the best teams, come from listening first.
