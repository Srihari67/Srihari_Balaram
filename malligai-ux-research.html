Minimal & sustainable fashion shopping

A UX case study on helping shoppers decide faster and with more confidence, so they buy what they intend to keep.

Goal
Reduce decision fatigue and promote mindful shopping

1. Decision fatigue is common

Platforms overwhelm users with too many options.

Seen in large catalogs and endless scrolling.

Opportunity: curated "Top Picks" with limited, relevant options.

2. Size confidence is missing

No competitor solves size and fit uncertainty well.

Basic size charts only, with no personalization.

Opportunity: a "Find My Size" feature and fit recommendations.

3. Weak personalization

Recommendations are generic.

Same products for everyone, with no context like event or style.

Opportunity: occasion-based and behavior-based suggestions.

4. Trust is built, not enhanced

Competitors rely on ratings and reviews alone.

No real-life validation.

Opportunity: real customer photos and "People like you bought this".

5. Checkout is functional, not delightful

Usable, but not optimized for speed or clarity.

Multiple steps and hidden costs.

Opportunity: one-page checkout with transparent pricing.

Strategic insight

There is a clear gap for a simple, confidence-driven shopping experience that helps users decide faster.

Browsing flow

  1. Open app
  2. Personalized home
  3. Select category
  4. Relevant?
  5. Yes: view products, open product
  6. No: refine filters

Friction: too many options. Curated results reduce overload.

Selection flow

  1. Open product
  2. View images
  3. Check reviews
  4. Size confident?
  5. Yes: select size
  6. No: use size tool
  7. Add to cart

Friction: size confusion. Size guidance improves confidence.

Checkout flow

  1. Open cart, review items
  2. Ready to buy?
  3. No: save for later
  4. Yes: checkout, check address, pay
  5. Payment success?
  6. No: retry. Yes: order confirmed

Friction: complex steps. Simple checkout reduces drop-offs.

Flow insight

Simplifying each step and reducing uncertainty leads to faster, more confident purchases.

Less browsing, better decisions

Key insights

  • Users struggle with decision-making, not discovery.
  • Size and fit uncertainty is the biggest barrier.
  • Too many options lead to decision fatigue.
  • Trust comes from reviews, real images and return policies.

Design approach

  • Simplified the shopping journey.
  • Focused on personalization and curated content.
  • Introduced features to improve size confidence.
  • Reduced friction in browsing and checkout.

Final outcome

A minimal, confidence-driven shopping experience that helps users make faster, better decisions online.

Sustainability angle

The design cuts unnecessary browsing and encourages intentional purchases, supporting more sustainable fashion consumption.
